Welome to the landing page for Borderland Keep dot com. Borderland Keep dot com hosts a variety of tools for online TTRPG play. These include a Matrix server, a Adventure XP calculator and a Weather generator. Both of the previous tools target the ACKS II system currently, but more systems are planned.

Matrix Homeserver - Status:

The Matrix homeserver, served from tavern.borderlandkeep.com, is intended for RPG players who wish to try Matrix as a possible alternative to discord. A general RPG channel and the tech support channel for this site are both hosted there. If you wish to sign up to have an account on the homesever, please contact the webmaster.
