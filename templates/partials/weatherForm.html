This page will generate monthly weather for ACKS II. You may use the server random number generator or supply your own dice rolls. The initial weather generated will always be for the Borderlands Region of the Auran empire in Summer (Cfa Climate, Easterly prevailing wind). If you would like have it auto-generate with different setting, simply bookmark the "quick-link" generated at the bottom of the controls section. If "Simulate weather system" is selected, the server will use method listed in the JJ for each day after the first. You may supply your own dice rolls if desired by selecting the "Manual" Generation mode. The "Foretell!" button re-rolls the entire month using the given parameters.
